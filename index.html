The app combines AI assistance, document reading, safety detection, voice navigation, SOS, volunteer support, and consent workflows in one mobile system. Watch demo video.

Watch Visionmate AI demo

Product scope

  • Built core workflows for AI chat, document reading, safety camera, voice navigation, SOS, volunteer support, and research consent.
  • Designed the mobile experience around Thai language support, TalkBack-aware labels, spoken feedback, haptics, and large-touch interaction.
  • Connected visually impaired users and trusted volunteers through chat, emergency alerts, location pinging, and call-signaling workflows.
  • Kept safety messaging conservative: the app supports awareness and navigation decisions but does not replace a white cane or trained mobility support.
